AI Tutor

A tutor that
knows your notes.

Upload your lecture slides, PDFs, and notes. The AI tutor answers questions using your actual class materials — not the whole internet, not a generic textbook.

  • Grounded answers with citations back to your notes
  • Generates practice quizzes from your material
  • Explains concepts at your level, not a professor's
  • Private to you — your notes aren't training data

Class Chat

One chat room
per class.

Real-time group chat scoped to your course — not another Discord server you'll forget about. Join with a class code and you're in.

  • Per-class rooms, auto-created when you add the course
  • Share notes, slides, and study guides directly
  • @-mention classmates for study-group coordination
  • Push notifications for replies, muted by default
