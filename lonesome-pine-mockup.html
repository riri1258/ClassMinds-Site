Our Story

A Bay Lake supper club, kept the way it should be.

For decades the Pine has been the place locals bring family, where weekenders end a long day on the lake, and where the smell of the grill drifts out through the screen door. Polished cedar tables, generous portions, neighbors at the bar.

We don't reinvent. We just keep it honest — fresh fish from up north, hand-cut steaks, pasta the way grandma made it, and a kids' playground out back so dinner runs a little longer.
