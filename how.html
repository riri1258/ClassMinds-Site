How It Works

From install to
studying in minutes.

Five steps. No data migration, no manual setup, no spreadsheets. Just download, add your classes, and start asking your AI tutor real questions.

1

Download from TestFlight

Tap the beta link, accept the TestFlight invite, and ClassMinds installs like any App Store app. No credit card, no sign-up page — just the app.

Tip: You'll need iOS 17 or newer. TestFlight is Apple's official beta testing app — completely safe.
2

Create your account

Sign in with Apple or email. Pick a display name for your class chats. That's the whole onboarding — no 12-step wizard.

Privacy note: Display name is visible only to classmates you share a class with. Your real name stays private.
3

Add your classes

Snap a photo of your schedule or type course codes manually. ClassMinds creates a hub, chat room, and deadline tracker for each class automatically.

4

Upload notes to feed the AI

Drop in lecture slides, PDFs, or your own notes. The AI tutor uses these to answer grounded questions — it cites the specific pages it pulled from, so you know where the answer came from.

What works: PDFs, images, typed notes, and pasted text. What's next: handwritten-note OCR is on the roadmap.
5

Ask, chat, and stay on top

Ask the tutor anything about your class. Coordinate with classmates in the class chat. Get reminded before deadlines. Repeat every week, never fall behind.

Pro move: Ask "quiz me on chapter 5" before an exam — the tutor generates questions from your actual notes.
