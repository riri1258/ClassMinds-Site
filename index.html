Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built specifically for college students juggling too many classes and not enough time.

🎓
AI Tutor
Your personal tutor that actually knows your notes and assignments. Ask it anything — it'll pull context from your real class materials.
💬
Class Chat
Real-time group chats for each of your courses. Coordinate with classmates, share notes, and never miss a group project update.

Class Hub
All your classes in one place. Track assignments, store notes, and see everything due at a glance — no more juggling tabs.
