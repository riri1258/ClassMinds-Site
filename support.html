Getting the most out of ClassMinds
Tip 01
Add notes early
The AI Tutor gets smarter the more notes you add. Add them right after class for best results.
Tip 02
Use class chat
Get classmates to join your class group so the chat is actually useful when it matters.
Tip 03
Ask specific questions
Instead of "explain ECON", try "explain Chapter 4 supply and demand from my ECON notes."
Tip 04
Check upcoming work
The home screen shows everything due this week. Check it every morning to stay on top of things.
