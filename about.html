About

Built by a student,
for students.

ClassMinds started as a simple idea — what if one app actually had everything a college student needs?

The Story

College is overwhelming. Between keeping up with multiple classes, tracking assignments, finding notes, and coordinating with classmates — it's a lot. Most apps solve one piece of the puzzle and leave you duct-taping the rest together.

ClassMinds was built to fix that. One app that holds your classes, connects you with classmates in real time, and gives you an AI tutor that actually knows your course material — not just generic textbook answers.

It started as a personal project and grew into something worth sharing. We're still early, still growing, and still listening to students to make it better.
